About Me

Building immersive experiences through gameplay and technology.

I'm UdayaShankar. My journey into game and software development began at Seamedu Pune, where I completed my BCA with a specialization in game development.

I worked at Vr2ality Pune and later at Immersivevision Technology, contributing to educational and simulation projects like Simulab and Cadaviz.

My expertise includes gameplay programming, parkour mechanics, optimization, shaders, editor tools, AI systems, and simulation frameworks.

I enjoy analyzing gameplay systems deeply and creating immersive mechanics that combine creativity with strong technical execution.
